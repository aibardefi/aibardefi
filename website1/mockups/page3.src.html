Page 3 mockup — you scrub it yourself

Drag the gold knob. The padlock's shackle lifts in step with your drag, the amount owed counts down, and the doors only part once you have paid every last $SB.

  • New mechanic on purpose. Page 1 was tapping, page 2 was one big trigger. This one is continuous — you control every frame, and it runs backwards if you drag back.
  • Let go early and it snaps back and he shakes his head. Part-paying gets you nothing, which is exactly how a loan works.
  • Finish it and the lock falls off, the doors swing open, and your five memes float back out to you.
  • Keyboard works too — focus the slider and use the arrow keys.
