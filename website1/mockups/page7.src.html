Page 7 mockup — the way out

He lands, tips his hat, gold puffs up behind him, and the three buttons arrive one after another. Then it stops moving and gets out of the way — this is the page where people are meant to click, not play.

  • Deliberately the calmest page. Six pages of toys, then a page that behaves. One orchestrated entrance on load, no game.
  • Launch app is the only gold thing here, so the action you actually want is the one thing that shouts — same trick as the red lever on page 2.
  • Contract address is a dashed, un-clickable Coming soon. The copy-to-clipboard is already wired — when you have the address, it becomes solid and copyable with one line changed.
  • Try it: click the address box now and it tells you it is not ready instead of doing nothing.
