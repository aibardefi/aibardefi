Page 4 mockup — whack the alarm

It is ringing when you arrive. Hit the clock. Each hit drags him one stage further out of bed — and the fifth one is where $SB gets invented.

  • Third mechanic, third feel. Page 1 accumulates, page 3 scrubs, this one is a five-state machine you punch through. The clock physically shakes while it rings, so it asks to be hit.
  • Stages: flat out → stirs → one eye → sits up, blanket thrown off → lamp on, "Enough. I want a different life."
  • Same background as every other page. The 6am read comes from the lamp, the blanket and the drifting z's — not from recolouring the room.
  • His shut eyelids are drawn over your artwork and fade off as he wakes, so the same file does asleep and awake.
