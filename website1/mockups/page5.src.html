Page 5 mockup — three jobs, three punchlines

Click each card. It lifts off the table, turns edge-on, and lands with the punchline arriving a beat later — the pause is the joke. Turn all three and he walks on to take the credit.

  • Fourth mechanic. Tap-to-collect, one-shot lever, drag-scrub, whack-the-clock — and now a reveal. Nothing here counts up or runs a timeline.
  • The flip is three moves, not one: lift and rotate 90° edge-on, complete the turn, settle. A single rotateY reads cheap; breaking it makes the card feel like an object.
  • The unturned cards drift out of sync with each other, so the row is never dead still.
  • 1st / 2nd / 3rd in the corners is real order — this is the sequence he actually went through.
