Page 6 mockup — the playground

He watches your cursor the whole time: eyes track it, body leans toward it, and his shadow squashes as he goes. Then press any of the six buttons.

  • Fifth mechanic, and the busiest page on purpose. Continuous pointer tracking plus six hand-timed one-shots — this is the page that exists to show off.
  • Every mood is timed by hand, not one shared wobble: the jump has anticipation, a squash on take-off and a landing; the spin winds back before it goes and overshoots 32° past before settling; rage runs on stepped frames so it judders instead of gliding.
  • Each one brings its own effects — dust on landings, a gold arc on the spin, steam on the rage, blush when shy, drifting z's for the nap.
  • Three nested layers carry pose, lean and breath separately, which is what lets him keep breathing and leaning while a mood plays instead of snapping to a stop.
