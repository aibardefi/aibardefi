Frequently asked question

Simple answers for degens before they touch the machine.

What is SB?

SB is a fixed supply memecoin with a native treasury-originated borrowing protocol on Robinhood Chain.

Does the apparatus mint SB?

No. SB already exists in treasury. The apparatus is only the visual metaphor for locking collateral and receiving a treasury loan.

Can any memecoin be collateral?

No. Only approved memecoins should be eligible, with oracle, liquidity, and risk checks.

Can users lose collateral?

Yes. If the position crosses the liquidation threshold, collateral can be sold to close the SB debt.
